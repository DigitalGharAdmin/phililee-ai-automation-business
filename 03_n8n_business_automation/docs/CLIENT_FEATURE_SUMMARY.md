# Business inquiries, handled consistently

Receive inquiries, check required information, route work and keep a tracking record.
Optional receipt emails use business-approved wording. AI assistance is optional;
your business rules remain in control.

- Intake and routing for sales, support, billing, complaints and general inquiries.
- Spreadsheet tracking with clear processing and email statuses.
- Optional acknowledgements, with review required for sensitive categories.
- Duplicate protection that reuses records without another email.
- Failure handling that flags uncertain delivery for operator review.
- Private credentials and alerts that exclude customer messages.

## What can be customized?

Business name, route labels, acknowledgement subject/body, email and AI switches,
default priority, operator alerts and response version. Real alert recipients and
credentials are configured privately during onboarding.

## What delivery involves

Agree the settings and wording, prepare the sheet, connect client-owned accounts,
then test no-send processing, an authorized acknowledgement, duplicates and recovery.
A named operator handles uncertainty. The demo does not automatically constitute
a production deployment or promise zero failures. See the
[handoff checklist](CLIENT_HANDOFF_CHECKLIST.md).
